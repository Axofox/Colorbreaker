Block Drop

Stack the blocks. Fill a row to clear it.

Blocks fall from the top. Move and turn them so they fit together.

Fill a whole row from side to side and it disappears. Clear more rows at once for more points. Every 10 rows the blocks fall faster.

The game ends when the stack reaches the top.
