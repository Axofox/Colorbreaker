Color Rides

Give everyone a ride.

With math

Colors: people walk round and round. Tap a car to drive it out the way its arrow points (only if nothing is in the way). It parks in one of the 4 spots, and people of its color get in as they walk past the exit. If all 4 spots are full and nobody fits, the game is over.

With math: a passenger is waiting with a number. Tap the car whose sum makes that number. The car picks them up! 3 wrong cars and the game is over.
