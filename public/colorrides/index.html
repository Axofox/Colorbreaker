Color Rides

Drive the cars out and give everyone a ride.

With math

Tap a car to drive it out the way its arrow points. It can only go if nothing is in the way. Then it waits in a parking spot.

Colors: people get on a car of their color. With math: each car has a sum, and people get on the car whose answer is their number.

A full car drives away. If all 5 spots are full and nobody can get on, try again.
