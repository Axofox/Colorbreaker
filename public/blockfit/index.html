Block Fit

Clear the star blocks to finish each scene.

Drag a shape from the bottom onto the grid. Or tap a shape, then tap the square where its top block should go.

Fill a whole row or column and it clears. Each scene starts with some blocks marked with a star. Clear every star block to finish the scene and get bonus points.

When all three shapes are used you get three new ones. The game ends when none of your shapes fit. How many scenes can you finish?
