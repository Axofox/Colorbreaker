Block Fit

Drag shapes onto the grid. Fill lines to clear them.

Drag a shape from the bottom onto the grid. Or tap a shape, then tap the square where its top block should go.

Fill a whole row or column and it clears. Clear lines one move after another for a combo with extra points.

When all three shapes are used you get three new ones. The game ends when none of your shapes fit.
