Mahjong Pairs

Find the pairs and clear the stack.

Tap a tile that isn't covered by another tile. It goes into the tray. Two tiles that are the same pop and disappear.

The tray holds 4 tiles. If it fills up with 4 different tiles, the game is over. Clear every tile to finish the stage.

Stuck? Use Shuffle, Hint or Undo.
