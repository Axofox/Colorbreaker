🔐 Color Breaker

Crack the secret color code!

How to play

The computer hides a secret code of colors. Pick colors to make a guess, then press Check.

⭐ = right color in the right spot
⚪ = right color in the wrong spot

Use the clues to crack the code before you run out of tries!

Easy: 4 spots, 5 colors, no repeats. Normal: 4 spots, 6 colors, repeats allowed. Hard: 5 spots, 6 colors, repeats allowed.
