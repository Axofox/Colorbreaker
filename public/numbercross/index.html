Number Cross

Fill in the missing numbers.

Drag a number into an empty box. Or tap the box, then tap the number. Tap a green number to take it back.

You have 3 tries. After 3 wrong numbers you get a new puzzle. You also get 2 hints.

Easy: plus and minus up to 10. Medium: up to 20. Hard: up to 30, with times 2 and divide by 2.
